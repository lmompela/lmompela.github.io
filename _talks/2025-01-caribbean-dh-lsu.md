This talk explores the challenges of developing an AI-enabled translation and interpretation app tailored to the Haitian Creole speaking population in Miami, including issues of linguistic variation, multilingual code-switching, and domain-specific vocabulary for medical settings. Join us to learn how cutting edge
natural language processing (NLP) technology can promote equity for Creole-language communities across the US.
